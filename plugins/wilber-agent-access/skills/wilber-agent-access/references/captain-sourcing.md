# Captain sourcing with Wilber

Use this reference when a Captain asks how to source founders, research prospects or manage the resulting private contacts. Current identity, returned tools and explicit connection permissions remain authoritative. Do not promise a research service or team workflow that their connection does not expose.

Wilbe already runs central online prospecting. Captains add particular value through local networks, conversations and introductions that online research misses. Help them capture those connections, update platform work and maintain their private CRM. Public research remains useful for finding local inroads and preparing conversations; do not present duplicating Wilbe's central prospecting run as their default task or start a team workflow on their behalf.

## Answer a how-to question

Explain the useful outcome first: Claude or Codex can turn local conversations into private contacts, notes and follow-ups, help with permitted platform work, and research local inroads when useful. It can use permitted Wilbe context to check relevant existing records and show the evidence before suggesting a next step. Public discovery uses the agent's available research tools; Wilber supplies permitted platform context and CRM operations. A working connection does not automatically start a sourcing campaign.

Use a current access check and tool catalog when they have not already been established in this task. Name the relevant capabilities in plain language. Permission counts and a long tool inventory usually add little to an introductory answer. If the helper works but the connector does not, explain that distinction once and use the permitted helper with the same credential; do not use a fallback to bypass a permission denial or claim the connector is repaired.

If the Captain only asks how it works, give a short practical example and ask one useful scoping question, such as which technical field and location to start with. Reuse an existing brief rather than asking for it again. Do not launch arbitrary research, an asynchronous job, a save or outreach from a how-to question alone. If they already request research with enough context, start that work without another planning approval.

An example opening, adapted to the tools actually available:

> I can turn your local connections and meeting notes into proposed CRM updates and follow-ups. I can also research local inroads and check the Wilbe context available to you before suggesting an approach. Do you have meeting notes to start with, or a particular field and location in mind?

Also adapt examples to their local work: "Turn my notes from yesterday's lab visit into proposed CRM updates and follow-ups" or "Help me find local inroads around this research group and check the Wilbe context before suggesting who to approach."

## Research a requested shortlist

Use the current Captain criteria from their brief and entitled Wiki when needed. Read the relevant guidance once; do not repeatedly summarize the Wiki instead of doing the requested research. Distinguish pre-company technical talent, a forming team and an established company. Funding, location and company-formation filters come from the actual brief and current criteria, not a universal rule inferred from an earlier example.

Check relevant own contacts, visible community or portfolio records and entitled campaign tools only where useful and available. State search coverage: no matching result in a paged or keyword search does not prove there are no contacts or portfolio companies in a whole field. Directory membership does not prove a Wilbe relationship or introduction route. Questions about who Wilbe knows or a current warm route follow the existing relationship-context gate, with its actual permission and confirmation requirements.

Use current public evidence when research tools are available. If they are unavailable, explain the limit and work with supplied evidence or permitted existing records; do not invent fresh research. Cite clickable source pages beside claims, including dates when freshness matters. Separate verified facts, fit judgment and uncertainty. Conflicting technical descriptions need reconciliation or a clear unresolved label. Failure to find a funding round, paper or company registration does not establish its absence.

Group people from the same team or project and make the number of distinct opportunities clear. Two cofounders can be useful contacts, but do not silently count them as two independent company opportunities. Keep uncertain technical talent as a separate watchlist when appropriate. Return fewer strong matches with a clear gap rather than padding a requested number with people whose role or fit cannot be supported.

## Check Wilbe context before recommending an approach

For a named shortlist, check the relevant prospecting and relationship context exposed by the current connection before describing someone as a new Wilbe lead or recommending contact. Match identity using confirmed profile links, organisation and source identifiers where available; a similar name alone is an unresolved match.

Use the Captain workspace to identify entitled campaigns, search those campaign records, and fetch the permitted candidate and relevant contact history when a match is found. Preserve returned holds, contact status, prior outreach, replies and known routing constraints. A private CRM duplicate check covers the Captain's own records; it is not a central prospecting check. A campaign search covers its authorized campaign, not all Wilbe prospecting.

When current Wilbe relationships, owners or warm routes are relevant, follow the skill's relationship-context gate with the actual permission and confirmation requirements. Inspect the returned source coverage and freshness. The current Captain relationship lane can return a limited professional-directory lead with coordinate-first routing; do not treat that as a check of central prospecting, correspondence or holds. A queued request is not a completed check, and not_checked, unavailable or unresolved results do not mean the person is clear to approach.

Keep a compact Wilbe-context note with each opportunity: known prospecting state, relevant prior contact and date, any permitted hold or routing instruction, a verified relationship owner when actually returned, and what was checked or remains unknown. Use only permitted summaries. If a hold is visible but its reason or owner is not, say that coordination with Wilbe is needed; do not invent or expose confidential explanations.

An empty, partial, unavailable or unauthorized lookup must remain "Wilbe context unknown" or "no match within the checked scope", rather than "Wilbe does not know them" or "not contacted". Do not present a new-lead or outreach recommendation as cleared while material context is unknown. Research and private note-taking can continue, with coordination as the next step. Surface known holds before suggesting an approach; do not silently remove a hold, reset contact state or create a competing outreach workflow. Saving a private contact does not clear any central hold.

## Turn reviewed research into useful contacts

Present enough context to choose: the person and team, technical contribution, current stage, fit, source links, uncertainty and a sensible next step. Resolve confirmed profile URLs from evidence; leave unknown email or LinkedIn values empty. Keep private community evidence within the Captain's entitled workflow.

An unsaved research shortlist is the default result of a research request. Show the proposed fields and which contacts are proposed for saving. An explicit instruction to save named, reviewed contacts already authorizes that save; do not ask the same approval again. Use the currently listed duplicate lookup before creating records, preserve source context and use stable retry keys. A duplicate warning requires choosing the existing record or reviewing a separate merge, never an automatic merge.

Private CRM saving, submitting a reviewed copy to Wilbe and contacting someone are separate actions. Sharing uses its own reviewed submission tool and returns a receipt; it does not imply outreach or introduction consent. Keep follow-ups as the Captain's private actions unless they instruct another permitted effect. Report what was actually saved, shared or sent, with receipts where available.
